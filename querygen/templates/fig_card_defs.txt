CARD TYPES: find_figure = someone wants an image or plot of a specific thing (a pathway diagram of named components, a micrograph of a named tissue feature, a survival curve for a named treatment and disease); finding = someone wants the result the figure shows (which group does better, how a value changes over time, where a protein localises); appearance = someone wants to know what something looks like (a lesion on a scan, a rash, a stained cell type, a colony on agar); composed = someone has this image in front of them and asks a question about it that the citing text answers (why the curve levels off, what the arrow marks, what the result means): write the need as the question they would ask while pointing at the image.
