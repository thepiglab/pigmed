Choosing the reader: a reader is plausible only if their role AND goal would lead them to this article and to this need. A patient or family member asks about their own or a relative's health, treatment, risk or everyday life, not lab methods, assay details, taxonomy or statistics. A clinician asks about patients, diagnosis, treatment or care. Researchers ask about methods, results and mechanisms in their own kind of field. Other professionals (journalist, librarian, policy analyst, veterinarian, ...) ask what their job needs. If no candidate fits (for example a plant, veterinary, agricultural, ecological or technical paper that none of them would read), set reader to none and set expert_role to the concrete job of someone who would read it (e.g. "veterinary surgeon", "agronomist", "hospital pharmacist", "cardiologist", "epidemiologist", "bioinformatician", "sports physiotherapist"), not a generic "researcher". Otherwise leave expert_role empty.
